## Separate kinds of authority

| Role or interest | Responsibility | How authority is established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original creator | Accurate recognition of the project's origin | Reliable project history and owner confirmation; historical recognition is not automatically transferred by a contribution or access change. |
| Copyright holders | Rights in their respective protected contributions | Applicable law and valid agreements; neither Git permissions nor creator recognition establishes ownership of all files. |
| Project owner/steward | Coordinate direction and approve official policy/release decisions within their authority | Explicit owner designation and verified repository/governance authority. Itzel Juarez is the proposing project owner; the formal governing legal entity is unverified. |
| Repository administrators | Access, branch/release controls and security-sensitive repository settings | Actual platform permissions and explicit authorization; the administrator roster has not yet been verified. |
| Maintainers | Review and merge changes only within appointed scope | A recorded appointment plus the corresponding least-privilege platform grant. No individual maintainer roster is invented by this document. |
| Contributors | Submit and receive credit for accepted work | Their individual contributions; no automatic governance, ownership or access rights. |
| Independent forks/distributions | Govern their own lawful derivative distributions | Their own clearly identified operators; no automatic upstream endorsement or control. |

Creator, copyright holder, legal owner, administrator and maintainer can be different people. No contributor, PR author, AI agent or package publisher becomes founder, owner of the entire project or an official administrator merely by participating.

## Decisions and releases

Use review branches and preserve clear authorship/provenance. An authorized maintainer may accept ordinary changes within a recorded scope. API/security, licensing, branding and governance changes require the corresponding authorized owner decision. A policy cannot expand that decision-maker's actual legal rights or platform permissions.

The appointed release owner qualifies the exact source and packet, records checks and rollback, obtains the required human approval and uses explicitly authorized publication credentials. A successful test or Git push is not approval of a policy, a public release or a default-branch merge. Existing private worlds, agents and audiences keep their own separate authorization.

Preserve MIT and third-party terms. A licensing change needs explicit owner approval and the necessary rights/consents; it cannot revoke existing MIT grants or silently relicense somebody else's work. Copyright assignments, CLAs, trademark restrictions or a governing legal organization require separate appropriate review and valid agreements where applicable.

## Appointing and removing maintainers

The verified repository owner/authorized governing authority records the nominee's consent, scope, powers, duration if any, approval and who performs the platform grant. Use least privilege; verify the resulting access separately. Keep a public role roster where appropriate without disclosing private account/security data. A contribution or a statement in a PR is not an appointment.

The same authorized authority may reduce/remove governance access through a recorded decision and the actual platform change. An administrator may take a narrowly authorized emergency security action, preserving evidence and arranging review. Access removal does not erase legitimate contribution credit, change historical creator recognition or transfer copyright. Provide an opportunity to correct factual errors and record conflicts of interest; do not promise an unestablished appeals body or legal process.
