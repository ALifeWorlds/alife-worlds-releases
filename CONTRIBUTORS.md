# Creator and contributor recognition

**Approved project recognition policy.** The human authorized publication on official main on 10 November 2026. Recognition is factual attribution, not an assignment of intellectual property or a grant of governance authority.

## Keeping recognition accurate

Accepted PRs and commits are the primary contribution record. Maintainers may add a concise description of an accepted contribution, a release acknowledgment and an optional contributor profile with that person's consent. Do not invent a profile, map a display name to an unverified legal identity, publish private contact details or credit a person for work not supported by records.

Corrections should cite the relevant commit, accepted PR, asset provenance or other reliable record. Preserve the prior record while resolving a dispute. An AI agent or automation may be disclosed as assistance where appropriate; do not name it as a verified human copyright holder or use it to manufacture legal authority.

Contribution recognition does not confer original-creator status, ownership of the project as a whole, repository access, release authority or a right to represent a fork as official. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
