## Original generated artwork and excluded history

The active packet uses four newly generated no-reference woodland atlases and the restored original ALife Worlds planet icon family confirmed by its human creator. The [art provenance](web/agent-outpost/assets/midnight-colony-reference-20261107/art-provenance.json) records new prompts and exact bytes. These project assets are offered under the main MIT terms to the extent rights exist; AI generation is disclosed and no exclusivity/non-infringement guarantee is asserted. Earlier reference-derived raster assets and unused legacy rasters are excluded from current distribution; private recoverable preimages and Git history are preserved. Public website art and Dot are not plugin payload.

The build checks the [exact media rights inventory](docs/media-rights.json): every distributed image/audio/font needs one matching source/license/hash record. Missing, changed or unsupported entries block packaging. The nine audio files retain their documented CC0 terms and creator records; their primary source listings were checked on 18 November 2026. MIT does not relabel third-party dedications or erase notices.

## Dependencies and tooling

The owner-Site template declares esbuild 0.28.2 as a development dependency. A declared dependency/version is not proof of its entire transitive license chain. Review actual dependency manifests/license files when dependencies are installed, vendored or redistributed, and carry their applicable notices. No third-party runtime package or paid service is introduced by this governance change.

## Review limits

This inventory preserves recorded notices; it is not an exhaustive legal ownership opinion. Individual members behind the collective notice, assignments/employment rights, source-reference rights and any contributor agreements remain verification items. New or changed license/dependency records are surfaced by `npm run attribution:check` for human review.
