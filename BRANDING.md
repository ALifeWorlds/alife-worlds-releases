# ALife Worlds branding and independent distributions

**Approved project-identification guidance.** The human authorized publication on official main on 10 November 2026. No registered trademark, exclusive logo right or new trademark license is asserted here. This guidance does not change [MIT](LICENSE) or applicable third-party licenses.

## Official identity

The project name is **ALife Worlds** and the owner-confirmed original creator is **Itzel Juarez**. ALife Worlds publishes official public packets at [ALifeWorlds/alife-worlds-releases](https://github.com/ALifeWorlds/alife-worlds-releases). Only releases approved through the verified official repository/release authority may be described as official releases. A matching version string, copied icon or contributor account does not establish that status.

Official release/presentation changes to the name, logos or creator credit need the authorized owner's approval. Preserve the existing icon assets and their provenance; this task neither redesigns them nor adds ownership notices to artwork whose rights have not been established.

## Forks and third-party distributions

Clearly identify an independent fork, its operator, its changes and its relationship to upstream. For example: “Independent fork of ALife Worlds, originally created by Itzel Juarez; maintained by [the actual fork operator]. No official endorsement is claimed.” Use only factual, verified statements. Do not falsely claim original creation, an official partnership or endorsement.

Retain the copyright and permission notices required by MIT and any applicable third-party notices. Creator acknowledgment is encouraged separately as project history; this guidance does not invent an extra MIT attribution requirement, a mandatory rename rule, a commercial-use ban or a prohibition on lawful copying, modification, distribution, sublicensing or sale.

Logo/name use follows the applicable existing licenses and law. No additional logo license or blanket reservation is created by this draft. Lawful descriptive/nominative reference and MIT-granted rights are not withdrawn. A request for official co-branding or endorsement requires actual owner authorization; silence or a merged contribution is not endorsement.

## Further protection needs separate review

Trademark registration/status, ownership, territorial scope, a separate trademark policy, logo licensing and remedies for misleading branding need verified facts and appropriate legal review. Nothing here claims those protections are automatically created by MIT, creator credit, Git hosting or this file. Do not use a registration symbol or claim registered protection without verification.
