# Help improve ALife Worlds

Use the bug or improvement form under Issues. Give the release version and a short example that others can reproduce with a sample world. The release maintainer reviews reports and publishes qualified packets. File additions, replacements and removals in the official distribution require maintainer authorization.

Contribute only material you own or may redistribute. Accepted code can be distributed under the project’s MIT license. Preserve existing copyright, license and attribution notices and identify separately licensed material. Contributors retain applicable rights to their original work. An accepted contribution grants credit for that contribution. Maintainer authority requires a separate appointment.

Itzel Juarez is the original creator of ALife Worlds. See [community conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), [branding](BRANDING.md) and [security reporting](SECURITY.md). The packet contains the complete source contribution policies and notices.
